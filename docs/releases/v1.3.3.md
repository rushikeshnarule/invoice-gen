# Invoice Gen v1.3.3 - US Edition, Multi-Tax Engine & High-Density UI

Invoice Gen v1.3.3 brings international tax versatility, dedicated US edition builds, and high-density workspace ergonomics.

### 🌟 Key Highlights
* **US Sales Tax & Multi-Tax Presets**:
  * Configurable US state-specific tax rates and custom sales tax handling.
  * Presets for standard Indian GST (CGST, SGST, IGST) alongside global flat sales tax rates.
* **Dedicated US Edition**:
  * Specialized US Edition builds with pre-configured US tax rules, currency symbols, and address formats.
* **Dual Architecture macOS Builds**:
  * Added dedicated Apple Silicon (`arm64`) and Intel (`x64`) standalone zip archives for macOS users.
* **High-Density Invoice Preview**:
  * Advanced zoom controls and crisp high-resolution invoice preview rendering.
* **Windows Portable & Installer Releases**:
  * Full Windows NSIS setup installers and standalone zero-install portable executables.
